About ResumeRanker

ResumeRanker is an AI-powered tool designed to help students and professionals evaluate the strength of their resumes in seconds. Our system scans your resume, identifies key technical and soft skills, and provides an accurate ranking based on relevance, keywords, and overall presentation.

Whether you’re applying for your first internship or aiming for a dream job, ResumeRanker helps you understand where your resume stands and how to improve it. We simplify the process of resume screening and make it accessible to everyone.

🎯 Our Mission

Empower every student and job seeker to create impactful resumes that get noticed. We believe in bridging the gap between skill and opportunity through smart technology.

🤝 Our Team

ResumeRanker is developed by a passionate group of students and developers who believe in using AI to solve real-world challenges in career development.
