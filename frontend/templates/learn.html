How ResumeRanker Works

Understand how our AI system evaluates and improves resumes.

💼 What is ResumeRanker?

ResumeRanker is an AI-powered platform that evaluates resumes against job descriptions. It helps candidates understand how well their resumes align with job requirements and provides personalized suggestions for improvement.

🚀 Step-by-Step Process

  1. Upload your resume (PDF or DOCX).
  2. Paste or upload your target job description.
  3. AI analyzes your skills, experience, and keywords.
  4. Generates a score (out of 100).
  5. Provides improvement suggestions instantly.

✨ Key Features

AI Keyword Matching

Compares your resume with job terms to identify alignment.

Skill Gap Detection

Highlights missing or underrepresented skills.

Ranking Score

Provides a quantified score to assess resume relevance.

Smart Suggestions

Recommends targeted improvements based on analysis.

🧠 Behind the Scenes

ResumeRanker uses Python and Natural Language Processing (NLP) techniques to analyze resume text. It applies TF-IDF and Cosine Similarity algorithms to evaluate how closely your resume matches the job description.

💡 Tips to Improve Your Resume

✅ Use job-related keywords throughout your resume.
✅ Showcase measurable achievements (e.g., “Increased sales by 30%”).
✅ Keep your format clean and consistent.
✅ Customize each resume for specific job roles.
